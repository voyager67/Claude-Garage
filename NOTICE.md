## What was adapted
Concepts such as spec-first workflows, complexity tiers with automatic dispatch, drift detection, the code ownership mandate for tests, adaptive multi-agent review, adversarial debugging with competing hypotheses, governance rules, output styles, a statusline and optional Agent Teams support. All text in this repository was written for Claude Garage, with names, structure and additions of its own.

## What is new in Claude Garage
The pitch-to-retro lifecycle, kill criteria and mandatory non-goals, the S, M and L lanes with blind acceptance scenarios, the Gremlin and Tester agents, `lessons.md` compounding memory, the interactive `/guide`, `/brainstorm` and `/rules` commands, the installer, and the interactive site.
